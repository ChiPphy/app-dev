# My Favorite Animation Movie: Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is a 2018 American animated superhero film that introduces a brand-new Spider-Man story through stunning animation and heartfelt storytelling. It was produced by Sony Pictures Animation and Marvel Entertainment.

**Main Characters**
### Miles Morales – A teenager who becomes the new Spider-Man.
### Peter B. Parker – The older and experienced Spider-Man who mentors Miles.
### Gwen Stacy (Spider-Woman) – A strong and confident Spider-hero from another dimension.
### Spider-Ham, Peni Parker, and Spider-Man Noir – Unique Spider-People from alternate universes.

**What I Love About It**
### “Anyone can wear the mask.”
### This quote sums up the movie’s inspiring message — that anyone can be a hero, regardless of who they are.

**My Favorite Song from the Movie**
### "Sunflower" by Post Malone & Swae Lee
### It perfectly matches Miles’ personality and the movie’s urban energy.
